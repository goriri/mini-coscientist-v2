# Co-Scientist Research Brief

**Question:** Find a testable hypothesis for improving battery cycle life

## Research-integrity notice

This draft contains proposed hypotheses, not verified findings. Verify literature, methods, ethics, safety, and statistical claims with qualified humans before action.

## Scope

Research objective: Find a testable hypothesis for improving battery cycle life

Constraints to confirm with the researcher:
- target system, population/material, and available measurements
- success metric, baseline, resources, timeline, safety/ethics

Deliverable: a falsifiable hypothesis and an auditable experiment plan.

## Generate

Candidate hypotheses (all require literature validation):
1. A controllable intervention changes the primary outcome by altering a measurable mechanism.
2. The intervention has its largest effect under a defined boundary condition.
3. A combined intervention outperforms either component through a testable interaction.

For each candidate, pre-register outcome, comparator, confounders, and a stopping rule.

## Reflect

Critical review:
- Novelty is unverified; search primary literature and negative results before claiming it.
- Causal claims need controls, randomization/blinding where applicable, and independent replication.
- Specify effect size, power calculation, preregistered exclusions, and adverse-event monitoring.

Recommendation: advance only candidates that remain feasible after these checks.

## Rank

Prioritization (provisional):
1. Candidate 1 — high tractability; mechanism and metric are directly observable.
2. Candidate 2 — potentially informative but boundary condition needs operational definition.
3. Candidate 3 — valuable if interaction is plausible; highest experimental complexity.

Scores are decision aids, not evidence: novelty 2/5, feasibility 3/5, expected impact 3/5, risk 3/5.

## Evolve

Refined lead hypothesis:
If the intervention is applied at a prespecified dose/window, the primary metric will improve versus matched control because the proposed mediator changes first.

Experiment: randomized matched-control pilot; measure baseline, mediator, outcome, and safety endpoints; analyze an effect estimate with uncertainty; then replicate independently.

Falsifier: no prespecified improvement, no mediator shift, or unacceptable safety signal.

## Proximity

Related-work map to validate:
- adjacent mechanism studies
- intervention and dose/window studies
- benchmark/control protocols
- contradictory or null-result reports

Do not infer novelty from this map. Attach DOI/PMID and exact supporting passages after a human literature review.

## Meta Review

Meta-review:
The lead is testable but not yet evidence-backed. Before execution, verify every factual claim and reference, obtain domain/safety/ethics review, define statistical analysis and data governance, and confirm resources.

Decision: CONDITIONAL ADVANCE — proceed only to protocol drafting, not scientific conclusion.
