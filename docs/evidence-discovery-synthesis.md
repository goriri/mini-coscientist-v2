# Co-Scientist Deep Research Synthesis & Annotated Bibliography Architecture

This document outlines the architecture for capturing, formatting, and presenting live online Vertex AI Deep Research literature discoveries in Co-Scientist V2 research dossiers.

---

## 1. Executive Synthesis (`DossierManifest`)
In Co-Scientist V2 (`workflow_version=2`), the **`meta_reviewer`** specialist agent evaluates the entire research lineage across all 8 stages:
- **`scope`**: Research objective and input sufficiency
- **`evidence`**: Iterative Deep Research passes and source verification
- **`generate`**: 10 diverse candidate hypotheses/models
- **`reflect`**: 5-axis independent reviews (`novelty_review`, `methods_statistics`, `impact_review`, `ethics_safety_governance`, `reflection`)
- **`rank`**: 3-round Swiss/Elo tournament and top-4 round robin
- **`evolve`**: Lineage evolution and refinement
- **`proximity`**: Related-work cluster mapping and diversity analysis
- **`meta_review`**: Final audit and recommendation

The output of `meta_reviewer` is normalized into a `DossierManifest` artifact. When `compile_dossier(session)` compiles the Markdown and ReportLab PDF dossiers, it renders `DossierManifest` into **`## Executive synthesis (Executive Summary)`**, displaying:
1. Top recommended candidate IDs and titles.
2. Unresolved fatal flaws and governance blocks.
3. Specific experimental evidence that would change the recommendation.

---

## 2. Deep Research Scientific Synthesis Report (`synthesis_report`)
When `IterativeEvidenceDiscovery.run` invokes the Vertex AI Deep Research transport (`deep-research-preview-04-2026`), it retrieves a full-text scientific literature survey and methodology analysis.
- The complete report text (`_extract_report(payload)`) is captured in `manifest.synthesis_report`.
- During dossier compilation (`_typed_summary("DiscoveryManifest", ...)` in `coscientist/dossier.py`), the full report is embedded under **`### Deep Research Scientific Synthesis Report`**.
- Clickable links to standalone readable artifacts are generated:
  - `deep_research_synthesis_report.md`
  - `deep_research_synthesis_report.pdf`

---

## 3. Annotated Bibliography & Source Evidence Mapping Table
To prevent Vertex AI Search grounding redirect URLs (`https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/...`) from obscuring scholarly titles, `_source_leads(report, ...)` in `coscientist/evidence.py`:
1. Extracts markdown link titles (`[Paper Title](url)`) from the Deep Research report text.
2. Maps inline citations and reference lists to canonical URLs.
3. Scans surrounding text paragraphs for contextual core findings and methodological relevance.

In the compiled dossier, source leads are formatted as an enterprise-grade Markdown Table:
```markdown
### Annotated Bibliography & Source Evidence Mapping Table

| # | Source Title & Clickable Link | Source Type | Core Finding & Methodological Relevance |
| ---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **[Solid-Electrolyte Interphase Engineering in Li-Ion Batteries](https://doi.org/10.1016/...)** | `primary or authoritative` | Demonstrates that fluoroethylene carbonate additives stabilize SEI layer morphology... |
```

---

## 4. Clickable Table of Contents & ReportLab Index of Figures and Tables
All PDF reports generated by `render_pdf(content)` feature:
- **Clickable TOC Bookmarks**: Section headings emit `#slug` destination anchors and ReportLab `BookmarkPage(slug)` flowables.
- **Visual Diagram Rendering**: Mermaid code blocks are rendered as styled ReportLab tables with header bars and flow arrows (`──▶`).
- **Two-Pass Page Number Resolution**: During Pass 1, `page_map: dict[str, int]` records the page number of every heading, figure, and table. During Pass 2, real page numbers are rendered in **`## Index of Figures and Tables`**.
